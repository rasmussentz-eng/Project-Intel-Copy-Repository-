Intel Sustainability

Building a greener future with every generation.

Discover Intel's sustainability milestones from founding innovation to modern climate leadership. Scroll the timeline for clean technology, renewable progress, and modern manufacturing goals.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore launch Intel, beginning a legacy of innovation and responsibility.

1971

First Microprocessor

The Intel 4004 arrives, setting the stage for smarter, more efficient computing across industries.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 processor launches the x86 era, powering desktops, servers, and future technology growth.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces 32-bit performance with the 386, creating new possibilities for computing and sustainability.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel identifies its emissions peak and begins investing in cleaner operations, energy efficiency, and renewable energy.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches the RISE goals, focusing on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction by 2030.

2022

Net-Zero by 2040

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions across global operations by 2040 for lasting climate impact.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, dramatically lowering its operational carbon footprint.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel brings partners together to accelerate sustainable semiconductor manufacturing and supply chain innovation.

Intel's Sustainability Vision

Renewable energy, water stewardship, and net-zero impact.

Intel continues to lead with responsible technology by setting bold goals for clean electricity, conserving water across global operations, and reducing emissions for a net-zero future.

Renewable energy goals

  • 100% renewable electricity for Intel operations
  • Scale energy efficiency at every site
  • Support clean energy programs globally

Water conservation efforts

  • Reduce freshwater use across facilities
  • Reuse water in manufacturing systems
  • Protect local watersheds and communities

Net-zero emissions commitment

  • Net-zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2040
  • Invest in low-carbon operations
  • Encourage climate action across suppliers
